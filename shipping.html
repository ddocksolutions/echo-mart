2. Shipping & Delivery

Echo Mart facilitates delivery of grocery and daily essential products through local vendors and delivery partners within selected service areas.

  • Delivery timelines are displayed at checkout.
  • Delivery charges, if applicable, are shown before order confirmation.
  • Delivery availability depends on vendor stock and service area.

3. Delivery Issues

  • If items are damaged, missing, or incorrect, customers must report within 24 hours.
  • Photographic proof may be required.
  • Resolution may include replacement or refund based on vendor approval.

4. Returns Policy

Due to the perishable nature of grocery items, returns are generally not accepted after delivery except in cases of:

  • Damaged items
  • Expired products
  • Wrong items delivered

5. Non-Returnable Items

  • Perishable goods after acceptance
  • Items damaged due to customer handling
  • Orders delivered successfully without complaint
