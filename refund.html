2. Nature of Platform

Echo Mart is a technology platform that facilitates ordering and delivery of goods from independent third-party sellers. Products are supplied by vendors. Echo Mart earns service/handling charges for facilitating the transaction.

3. Order Cancellation

  • Orders may be cancelled before confirmation by the vendor.
  • Once the order is confirmed and processed, cancellation may not be possible.
  • Perishable grocery items cannot be cancelled after dispatch.

4. Refund Eligibility

  • Refunds are applicable for incorrect, damaged, or missing items.
  • Issues must be reported within 24 hours of delivery.
  • Photographic evidence may be required for verification.

5. Refund Process

  • Approved refunds will be processed within 5–7 business days.
  • Refunds will be credited to the original payment method.
  • In case of Cash on Delivery, refund may be processed via bank transfer or UPI.

6. Non-Refundable Situations

  • Change of mind after delivery
  • Incorrect address provided by customer
  • Failed delivery due to customer unavailability
